We also talked about the possibility of participating in our Host Weekend Sleepover program! This program runs most weekends, giving our long-term residents a much-needed break from the shelter. Here’s what to know before you sign up:

  • Signing up: Complete the Host Weekend sign-up form each weekend you’d like to participate. You’re welcome to request a specific dog, but we can’t always honor requests — factors like home compatibility, a dog being adopted earlier in the week, or the need to rotate dogs who haven’t had a break in a while may affect placement.
  • Pick-up & drop-off: Pick-up is Friday evenings between 7:00–9:00 pm, and drop-off is Sunday around 7:00 pm or Monday around 12:00 pm — all in the Saving Grace parking lot. You’ll receive an email with your assigned time slot before pick-up.
  • Communication: Pickup info is sent the night before or morning of from host@savinggracenc.org — save this address to your contacts and check your spam/junk folder if you don’t see it by Friday morning.
  • Location: Participants must live within 1 hour of our Wake Forest location (13400 Old Creedmoor Rd).
  • Medical: Some dogs may be on medications (e.g., antibiotics for kennel cough). We recommend that your resident pets are up to date on vaccinations.

For full program details, guidelines, and FAQs, visit savinggracenc.org/host-a-dog.
